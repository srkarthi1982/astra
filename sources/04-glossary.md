# Glossary

## Approved

Partner-approved after Astra review and manual verification. Codex cannot approve.

## Astra

AI Partner and Senior Architect responsible for architecture, standards, verification, long-term planning, and code review guidance.

## Certification

Codex-led validation that an app passes automated checks, exploratory E2E, Playwright regression, production smoke where requested, and defect closure. Certification is not live approval.

## ComingSoon

Catalog state for apps that are not live promoted. A coming-soon app may be workflow-ready but is not approved for live user availability.

## Destination

The approved mature product direction recorded in `destination.md`. Destination metadata and Journey Progress come only from approved destination documents.

## Journey Progress

Product maturity and alignment with the approved destination, measured out of 100. It may increase, stay the same, or decrease after governance review.

## Live

Approved production launch state after promotion. Live requires Partner/Astra approval, production migration verification, Apps row update, metadata sync, and production verification.

## Market Study

Research document that captures external market intelligence, competitor patterns, pain points, UX patterns, pricing signals, AI trends, risks, and opportunities. It is not a backlog.

## Mini App

One Ansiversa solution app mounted under `/<app-slug>` inside the parent shell.

## Platform

The parent Ansiversa shell and ecosystem: authentication, routing, navigation, global search, user context, layout, and shared experience.

## Promotion

The approved process that moves an app from coming-soon/workflow-ready into live production status.

## Story

The current implementation memory in `story.md`. It explains what the module does today, how it works today, and why it is currently designed that way.

## Workflow Ready

The app has a real protected workflow, core user journey, persistence where required, documentation, validation, and catalog readiness, but may still be coming-soon and not live approved.

## WR

Short form for Workflow Ready.
