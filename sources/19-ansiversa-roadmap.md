# Ansiversa Roadmap

## Confirmed Roadmap

1. Complete the 101-product E2E certification campaign.
2. Complete Karthik manual acceptance and live promotion for approved apps.
3. Improve horizontal maturity per app after certification baselines are stable.
4. Add payment gateway after product quality gates are complete and approved.
5. Add AI agents after architecture approval.
6. Build marketing assets and 101 app videos after product readiness improves.
7. Prepare AI SEO documentation before implementation.
8. Implement AI SEO only after documentation and approval.
9. Add analytics and user feedback loops.
10. Continue replacements and enhancements only when existing apps no longer earn their place or need maturity improvements.

## Explicitly Not Planned Yet

- Driver.js implementation is not approved as an active implementation task.
- App #101 or App #102 expansion is not planned by default.
- Opportunistic AI features inside mini apps are not approved without architecture direction.
- Production live promotion for coming-soon workflow-ready apps is not automatic.
